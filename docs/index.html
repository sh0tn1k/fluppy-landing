About Fluppy

An AI assistant that lives inside Telegram — so collecting, rewriting, approval, and publish stay where you already work.

What is Fluppy?

Fluppy is an AI assistant for Telegram channel owners. It lives inside Telegram as a Mini App and bot — so you connect a channel, configure how it should write, and keep drafting and approval in one place.

Collect & rewrite your way

Pick the sources you want Fluppy to watch. Then set topics, language, posting frequency, and style — Fluppy rewrites drafts to match those settings before anything is ready to publish.

Approval stays with you

Drafts arrive for your review — typically via Telegram forums. Edit, schedule, or reject with a tap. The primary flow: you approve every post before it goes live.

Practical & private

Built for creators who want a clear workflow, not hype. Only what’s needed to run your channel — no tokenomics games, no invented vanity metrics.

How it works

Four steps from connect to publish — always with you in the loop.

01

Connect

Link Fluppy in Telegram and connect the channel you run.

02

Configure

Set topics, language, posting frequency, and style — plus sources to collect from.

03

Collect & rewrite

Fluppy gathers from your sources and formats drafts to your settings.

04

Approve & publish

Review drafts, edit or reject, then publish or schedule when you’re ready.

  1. Connect your channel

    Open Fluppy in Telegram, connect the channel you run, and add the bot as an admin so it can help with publishing.

  2. Configure topics, style & sources

    Choose what to collect, then set language, posting frequency, and style. Optionally use forums for the approval workflow.

  3. Collect, rewrite & send drafts

    Fluppy pulls from your sources and rewrites to your settings — ready as drafts for review, not live posts.

  4. Approve, schedule, or publish

    Edit wording, reject what doesn’t fit, schedule a slot, or publish when it feels right — you decide.
