# About Fluppy

Fluppy is an **AI assistant for Telegram channels**. It works inside Telegram as a **Mini App and bot** and helps channel owners automate repetitive content work without giving up control of what gets published.

## What Fluppy does

Fluppy automates the workflow behind a Telegram channel:

1. **Find relevant content** from the topics and sources you choose. You can use Fluppy's available sources or add your own feeds, channels, pages, and links.
2. **Rewrite posts in your style** using the language, tone, and format you configure for the channel.
3. **Prepare drafts for publishing** so content can be published immediately or on a schedule.
4. **Choose how much control to keep**:
   - **Approval mode:** drafts are sent to a Telegram forum so you can edit, reject, schedule, or publish them before they go live.
   - **Autopilot:** Fluppy publishes according to the channel's configured topics, style, and schedule.

Topics and sources are separate choices. Approval and autopilot are two publishing modes, so the channel owner decides how much automation to use.

## Key capabilities

- Telegram channel content automation
- AI-assisted rewriting of source material
- Topic-based content collection
- Custom sources, feeds, channels, pages, and links
- Language, tone, format, and writing-style controls
- Publishing frequency and scheduling
- Telegram forum approval workflow
- Autopilot publishing
- Recurring series and polls

## Built-in topics

Fluppy currently offers built-in topics such as AI, finance, health, psychology, sports, esports, startups and business, travel, facts, space, memes and video, crypto, football, X news, and Telegram news. Users can also create custom topics.

## Who Fluppy is for

- **Channel owners** who want consistent publishing with less manual research and rewriting.
- **Agencies** running repeatable content workflows across client channels.
- **Solo creators** who want faster drafts while keeping their own voice.

## How to start

Open Fluppy in Telegram, connect your channel by adding the bot as an administrator, choose topics and sources, configure language and style, then choose approval or autopilot.
