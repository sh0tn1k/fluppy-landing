# About Fluppy

Fluppy is a **Telegram Mini App and bot** that helps people run Telegram channels without doing every post by hand.

## What it does

Fluppy takes care of the repetitive part of running a channel:

1. **Find content** from the topics and sources you choose (Fluppy’s sources or your own feeds, channels, and pages).
2. **Write posts in your style** using the language, tone, and format you define.
3. **Prepare drafts for publishing** — post now or on a schedule.
4. **Publish with the control you want**:
   - **Approval mode:** drafts go to your Telegram forum; nothing publishes until you approve.
   - **Autopilot:** Fluppy publishes according to your topics, style, and schedule.

Topics and sources are separate choices. Approval and autopilot are equal modes.

## Who it’s for

- Channel owners who want consistent posting with less manual research
- Agencies running repeatable workflows across client channels
- Solo creators who want faster drafts while keeping their voice

## How to start

Open Fluppy in Telegram, connect your channel (add the bot as an admin), choose topics and sources, set language and style, then pick approval or autopilot.
