About Fluppy

An AI assistant that lives inside Telegram — so collecting, rewriting, approval or autopilot, and publish stay where you already work.

What is Fluppy?

Fluppy is an AI assistant for Telegram channel owners. It lives inside Telegram as a Mini App and bot — so you connect a channel, configure how it should write, and keep drafting and approval in one place.

Collect & rewrite your way

Pick the sources you want Fluppy to watch. Then set topics, language, posting frequency, and style — Fluppy rewrites drafts to match those settings before anything is ready to publish.

Approve — or go autopilot

Default: drafts land in a Telegram forum for review — edit, schedule, or reject before publish. Prefer hands-off? Switch on autopilot and Fluppy publishes by your topics, sources, style, and schedule. Turn it off anytime.

Practical & private

Built for creators who want a clear workflow, not hype. Only what’s needed to run your channel — no tokenomics games, no invented vanity metrics.
