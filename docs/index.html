    Choose ready-made topics or create your own. Add the sources you want Fluppy to follow.

  3. 03 — Set your style

    Choose the language, tone, posting frequency and other rules for your channel.

  4. 04 — Get ready-to-publish posts

    Fluppy collects relevant content and turns it into drafts that match your settings.

  5. 05 — Approve or automate

    Review every draft in Telegram, or turn on autopilot and let Fluppy publish for you.
